From photo to post in four steps

Most of the typing is already done by the time you read it.

1

Snap

Take a photo or pick one from your gallery.

2

Identify

AI reads the photo and fills in brand, model, category, condition and key specs.

3

Write

Get a title and description in your choice of style. Edit anything, then copy it.

4

Track

Save it to your ledger. Mark it sold and see your profit.

What you get

📷

Photo identification

A vision model reads the item and suggests brand, model, condition and specs so you don't have to look them up.

✍️

Marketplace-ready copy

A hook, spec bullets, a condition note and a call to action, written for Facebook Marketplace. Fully editable.

🖼️

Optional product shot

Generate a clean studio-style image of the item, free with Pollinations or with Gemini Imagen if you add a key.

📋

Listing ledger

Keep every item with its platform, status, list price and sold price, with totals for what you've earned.

🖨️

Reports and backup

Print or save a PDF report, and export or import your ledger and settings as JSON.

🔒

Private by design

Your ledger, photos and keys are stored on your device. Photos go to the AI provider only when you scan.

Bring your own keys

ListFast has no server and no subscription. Add your keys once in Settings (⚙).

Required

Groq API key

Powers photo identification and listing text. Free tier available from Groq.

Optional

Gemini API key

Only needed if you want Gemini Imagen to make product shots. Pollinations works without a key.
